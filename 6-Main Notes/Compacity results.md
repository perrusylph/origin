# Compacity results

## Theorem 1.4.4
- In Hausdorff spaces, compact subspaces are closed.
- If the space is also compact, every closed subspace is compact.

## Corollary 1.4.3

Compact Hausdorff spaces are __regular__ hence __normal__.

## Theorem 1.4.8

Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space. These are equivalent:
1. $X$ is compact.
2. $X$ is complete and totally bounded.

## Corollary 1.4.4

A metric space $(X,d)$ is __totally bounded__ if, and only if, its completion $(\hat{X},\hat{d})$ is __compact__.

## Theorem 1.4.9

Let $(X,d)$ be a __complete__ metric space. A subset $K$ of $X$ is __relatively compact__ $(\overline{K}\text{ is compact})$ iff it is __totally bounded__.

## Theorem 1.4.10

Let $(K_{\alpha})_{\alpha \in \Gamma}$ a family of compact topological spaces. Then the product $\Pi_{\alpha \in \Gamma}K_{\alpha}$ is compact.

## Definition 1.4.8

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a Hausdorff topological space. A family of subsets $(K_{\alpha})_{\alpha \in \Gamma}$ of $X$ is said to have the finite intersection property (FIP) if for every finite subset $F \subseteq \Gamma$,  $\bigcap_{\alpha \in F}K_{\alpha} \neq \emptyset$.

## Theorem 1.4.11

A Hausdorff topological space $(X,\tau)$ is __compact__ iff for any family $(K_{\alpha})_{\alpha \in \Gamma}$ of closed subsets of $X$ with FIP it happens that $\bigcap_{\alpha \in \Gamma}K_{\alpha} \neq \emptyset$.
