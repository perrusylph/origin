## Lattice
A lattice in the complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ is a subgroup which is [[Free group |free]] of dimension 2 over $\mathbb{Z}$, and which generates $\mathbb{C}$ over the reals. If $\omega_{1},\omega_{2}$ is a basis of a lattice $L$ over $\mathbb{Z}$, then we also write $L = [\omega_{1},\omega_{2}]$. Such a lattice looks like this:
![[Pasted image 20250609115523.png]]
Unless otherwise specified, we also assume that $\mathrm{Im}\left( \frac{\omega_{1}}{\omega_{2}} \right)>0$, i.e. that $\frac{\omega_{1}}{\omega_{2}}$ lies in the upper half plane $\mathbb{H}^+ = \{x+iy,y>0\}$.
