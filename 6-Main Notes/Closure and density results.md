# Cool closure results

## Lemma 1.4.1 (Brito)

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space and $U$ a non-empty open subset of $X$. If $A\subseteq X$ is such that $U \cap A = \emptyset$, then $U \cap \overline{A} = \emptyset$. In particular, if $U$ and $V$ are open, non-empty, disjointed sets, then $U \cap \overline{V} = \emptyset = \overline{U} \cap V$.

## Definition 1.4.2

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space and let $D$ be a subset of $X$. We say $D$ is __dense__ in $X$ if $\overline{D} = X$.

In general, if $A$ and $B$ are subsets of $X$ we say $A$ is __dense__ in $B$ if $B \subseteq \overline{A}$. This can also be expressed saying that if $V$ is a non-empty open subset of $X$ such that $V \cap B \neq \emptyset$, then $V \cap A \neq \emptyset$.

## Theorem 1.4.2

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space and let $D$ be a subset of $X$. Then, $D$ is __dense__ in $X$ if and only if for every non-empty open subset $U$ of $X$, $U \cap D \neq \emptyset$.

## Corollary 1.4.1

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space. If $D$ is dense in $X$ and $U$ is a non-empty open subset of $X$, then $\overline{U} = \overline{U \cap D}$.
